# Product demonstration images

These are authored landing-page illustrations derived from the real EVENTOUCH
Next.js product using a synthetic “파트너 컨퍼런스 데모” event in an isolated,
temporary PostgreSQL/API fixture on 2026-10-27. They are not real customer records,
delivery evidence, or screenshots of an operational deployment. The sample QR
belongs to a discarded test fixture and cannot grant entry.

The raw browser captures remain outside the repository. Each shipping PNG embeds
its source, crop and resize information. No generated imagery or reference-product
screenshots are used as product assets. The customer attendee banner is unchanged.

- `ev-console.png`: overview, resized viewport crop.
- `ev-report.png`: report workspace, resized full-page view.
- `ev-attendee-event.png`: attendee-home viewport.
- `ev-ticket.png`: synthetic ticket viewport.
- `ev-staff.png`: staff-work viewport.
- `../landing/ev-report-mobile.png`: report list and registration/entry metrics crop.

Current product verification and its limits belong to `docs/STATUS.md` only.
